## Everything your home needs to look after itself.

Six services, one team, one app. Each system works alone or together with the rest.

### Smart Home

Control lights, air conditioning, curtains and scenes from your phone or by voice, at home or away.

### Security System

Door and window sensors, motion detection and sirens that alert your phone the moment something happens.

### Camera Surveillance

HD indoor and outdoor cameras with live view, recorded playback and night vision, on your phone anywhere.

### Audible / Visual Intercom

See and speak to visitors at the door, from inside the house or from your phone, and open the door remotely.

### Irrigation System

Automatic watering schedules by zone, adjusted from the app, so your garden stays green and water isn't wasted.

### Garage / Access Door Control

Open and close garage doors, gates and access doors with a tap, with a log of who came and went.
